# Data sources and terms

The root MIT licence covers this repository's author-owned code. It does NOT relicense upstream polymer structure data or supersede upstream conditions. Keep primary_source and sources when reusing examples.

- PI1M: https://github.com/RUIMINMA1996/PI1M . Its MIT repository notice and README are preserved in licenses/. The README states "Data for academic purpose only." These limited research examples are not presented as unrestricted commercial data.
- OMG: https://github.com/TheJacksonLab/OpenMacromolecularGenome . The upstream GPL-3.0 notice is included in licenses/OMG-GPL-3.0.txt. Original data release: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7556992 . No replacement data licence is asserted here.
- SMiPoly: https://github.com/PEJpOhno/SMiPoly . The BSD-3-Clause notice is included in licenses/SMiPoly-BSD-3-Clause.txt.

The structures are source-attributed examples from existing datasets. They are not newly synthesized structures or experimental property labels. The collection name does not replace citation of the upstream PI1M, OMG and SMiPoly publications.

The endpoint-summary CSV reports author-side computational results, not upstream source-table records. It is released under CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Attribute PolyWeave-FS contributors and retain the experiment/scale/arm/seed columns and the scope explanation. Do not merge distinct experimental reference sets or interpret SD as a confidence interval.
